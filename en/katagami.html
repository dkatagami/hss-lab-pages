DAISUKE KATAGAMI / Ph.D.

Daisuke Katagami

Information Technology Course, Department of Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Tokyo Polytechnic University
Professor / Director of Academic Affairs, Faculty of Engineering
Ph.D. in Engineering

Through interactions between people, agents, and robots, I study intelligent systems that can live alongside people of diverse personalities.




Biography

  • 1992 — Graduated from Saitama Prefectural Kasukabe High School
  • 1996 — Graduated from the Department of Mechanical and Control Engineering, Faculty of Electro-Communications, the University of Electro-Communications
  • 1999 — Completed the master's program in Computational Intelligence and Systems Science, Interdisciplinary Graduate School of Science and Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology
  • 2002 — Completed the doctoral program in Computational Intelligence and Systems Science, Interdisciplinary Graduate School of Science and Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology Ph.D. in Engineering
  • 2002 — Research Associate, Department of Computational Intelligence and Systems Science, Interdisciplinary Graduate School of Science and Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology
  • 2004–2007 — Part-time Lecturer, Faculty of Environmental and Information Studies, Musashi Institute of Technology (concurrent appointment)
  • 2006 — Visiting Researcher, University of Hertfordshire, UK (concurrent appointment)

  • 2010– — Associate Professor, Department of Applied Computer Science, Faculty of Engineering, Tokyo Polytechnic University
