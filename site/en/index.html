01 / OUR VISION

Thinking about technology
through human relationships.

As AI becomes part of everyday life,
how it relates to people matters.

The Human Symbiotic Systems Laboratory studies agents and robots that interact flexibly with people of diverse personalities. Through dialogue, trust, and sociality, we explore a world in which people and intelligent systems live together.

A message from Daisuke Katagami ↗

PROFESSOR / MESSAGE

AI that adapts
to the diversity of people.

Professor, Information Technology Course, Department of Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Tokyo Polytechnic University
Daisuke Katagami / Ph.D. in Engineering

As agents and robots enter our living spaces, new forms of interaction between people and AI are emerging. The way we design these relationships shapes how we can live together.

Our research aims to enable autonomous agents and robots to live alongside people of diverse personalities and interact with them flexibly.
